HOW TO OPEN THIS ON A MAC
=========================

Works on Intel and Apple Silicon Macs.

This app isn't registered with Apple, so the Mac blocks it the first time.
That's expected. Here's how to get past it (one time only):

1. Double-click the app, or the "Start" file if there is one.
   (Optional: drag an app into Applications first.)
2. The Mac says it "can't be opened" or "can't be verified".
   Click "Done" (not "Move to Trash").
3. Open System Settings -> Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom,
   and click "Open Anyway" next to the app's name. Enter your Mac password.
4. Click "Open Anyway" once more. The app starts.

After that it opens normally with a double-click.

On older macOS: right-click the app, choose Open, then click Open.

If none of that works, open the Terminal app, type this with a space at
the end (don't press Enter yet):

    xattr -cr

then drag the app onto the Terminal window, press Enter, and double-click
the app again.
